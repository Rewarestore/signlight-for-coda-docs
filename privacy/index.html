Privacy policy

Signlight for Coda is a Pack published under the Reware Apps brand and operated by Reware ApS. It calculates a row fingerprint and a traffic-light sign-off status without sending your doc data to Reware.

Data handled by the Pack

The Pack receives only the formula inputs you select in the doc. It returns a deterministic fingerprint and a status object. Signer, time, signed fingerprint and revocation state remain ordinary values in your Coda table.

No Reware Apps storage or egress

The Pack declares no authentication, persistent Pack storage, analytics or external network domains. Reware Apps does not receive, copy or host your row values, fingerprints or sign-off records.

Superhuman Docs as the platform

Your doc and its values remain subject to your Superhuman Docs (formerly Coda) workspace access, retention and security settings, and to the platform's own terms and privacy commitments. Removing a row, table, doc or the Pack is controlled in Superhuman Docs.

Your choices and requests

You choose the fields passed to the Pack and can remove its formulas and buttons at any time. For privacy questions about Signlight, contact Reware Apps.
